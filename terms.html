Tuning is at your own risk

Slakee shows tunings and where their notes fall. It does not know your instrument, its neck, its setup or its strings, and it does not tell you whether a tuning is safe for them. Tuning any string above the pitch your instrument is set up for adds tension, and too much tension can break strings or damage the instrument.

Many tunings, especially those far from standard, need strings of different gauges (weights) to play and hold safely. Slakee does not recommend gauges or string sets for your instrument; choosing strings that suit a tuning is your responsibility. You decide what to play, which strings to use and how to tune — if you have any doubt, ask a qualified luthier first.
